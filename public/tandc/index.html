Who can use SpeedCash

You must be at least 18, live in Ghana, and use a Ghanaian mobile number that you control. You must complete your profile and identity check before we will consider a loan. We can refuse an account or an application if the information is incomplete, inconsistent, or fails our review.

Your account

You sign in with your phone number, an SMS OTP, and a 4-digit PIN. That PIN is yours. Do not share it, and do not let someone else use your phone to borrow in your name. Tell us at once if you think the account has been opened or used without you.

Keep your number, email, and profile current. We send OTPs, due-date reminders, and payout notices to the phone on the account. If that number is wrong, you may miss a disbursement or a repayment window.

You may change your PIN from Profile. You may log out at any time. Logging out does not cancel a loan you already took.

Identity check

Before you can apply, you submit personal details, work or school information, emergency contacts, and ID. We accept a Ghana Card, passport, voter card, or driver licence. You upload a photo of the front and back of the document and a selfie. Our team uses those files to confirm you are the applicant.

Emergency contacts are people you type in yourself. They are not automatically taken from your phone book, and they are not co-signers just because you listed them. We may call them if a loan is overdue and we cannot reach you.

If the ID, selfie, or details do not match, we will not approve the application.

Applying for a loan

When your profile is in, Home and Apply show the amount you can request. That range depends on your level with us. Submitting an application is a request, not a promise. Our team reviews it. We may approve it, reduce it, or turn it down.

You cannot take a new loan while another SpeedCash loan is still active. After you repay in full, a higher level may become available on the next offer. That is not automatic and not guaranteed.

Before you confirm, the app shows the amount, what you will repay, the due date, and any fee we are charging for that product. Read that screen. Confirming it is your acceptance of those figures.

How money is sent

Approved loans are paid to the mobile money wallet tied to your phone. We do not disburse to banks. If the wallet name, network, or number is wrong, the payout can fail or go to the wrong person. That error is yours unless we sent it to a number we stored incorrectly on our side.

A loan that is approved but still waiting for the mobile money transfer is already a live loan. You should not apply again while it is in that state.

Interest, fees, and what you owe

The repayment amount on your loan screen is what you agreed to pay. It already includes the interest and fees for that loan. We will not add a charge that was not shown to you, except a late penalty if you go past the due date, and an extension fee if you choose to extend.

SpeedCash loans are short-term digital credit. Amounts and tenors follow what the app shows you for that offer and what Ghanaian law allows for this kind of product.

Repaying by mobile money

You repay in the app from the active loan card. Payment is by mobile money through our payment gateway. We do not take bank transfers, cards, or direct debit.

You can pay the full balance or, where the app allows it, a partial amount. The outstanding figure updates after the payment is confirmed. A payment is not complete until the gateway and our system both mark it successful. If your wallet has no float, the attempt will fail and the due date does not move.

You may settle early. The amount to clear the loan is the outstanding balance plus any penalty already on the account, as shown in the app at the time you pay.

Extensions

If the loan is not yet overdue and the app offers an extension, you can buy extra days. The fee and the new due date are shown before you pay. That fee is collected by mobile money. Extending does not wipe the principal; it moves the deadline and adds the fee we displayed.

Late payment

If the due date passes without a full settlement, the loan is overdue. A daily penalty may be added to the outstanding balance at the rate used by the app for your country. You will see that penalty on the loan card.

While a loan is overdue we can refuse a new application, keep calling or texting the number on the account, and, if we cannot reach you, call an emergency contact you listed. We will not post your debt in public, threaten you, or pretend to be the police or a court.

Missed payments can affect whether we lend to you again and, where the law allows, may be reported to a licensed credit bureau.

Records

The Records tab is your history of loans and payments. Treat those entries as the running statement of the account. If a repayment shows in your mobile money wallet but not in SpeedCash, send us the reference and we will trace it.

Closing the account

You can delete your profile yourself: Profile, then Delete Profile. We will not delete an account that still has an outstanding or active loan. Pay it first, then delete.

Deleting the profile removes the customer record from our live systems. It does not forgive money you already owed. We may keep a thin legal file — that a loan was granted, repaid, or closed — where Ghanaian law requires us to keep it.

What we collect, in short

How we handle personal data is set out in the SpeedCash Privacy Policy. In brief we keep your phone number, PIN, email, profile, ID images, selfie, emergency contacts, loan figures, and mobile money payment records because we cannot lend or collect without them. We do not sell that file, and we do not scrape your SMS or your full contact list.

Messages from us

We will send OTPs, loan status, payout notices, due reminders, and replies to support on the phone or email attached to the account. Those messages are part of running the loan. If we ever send marketing, you can tell us to stop. Transaction messages will continue while you have an account or a balance.

Using the app fairly

Do not apply with someone else’s ID or number. Do not fake income, contacts, or photos. Do not try to open a second account to dodge an existing balance. Do not attack, copy, or disrupt the app. If we believe an application is false or the account is being used to defraud us, we can freeze it and report the matter.

When the app is down

Mobile networks, mobile money operators, and our own servers sometimes fail. We will restore service as soon as we can. A network outage does not cancel a loan that was already disbursed, and it does not extend a due date by itself. If a payment was deducted from your wallet during an outage, keep the receipt and contact us.

Our liability

We are responsible for paying a loan to the mobile money number we have on your profile, and for recording repayments that actually reach us. We are not responsible for money sent to a number you typed wrongly, for a PIN you shared, or for a phone you lost and did not lock. Nothing in this page takes away a right you have under Ghanaian law that cannot be signed away.
