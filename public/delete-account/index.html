SpeedCash is the lending app published on Google Play by PRYNOVA.NET. This page is how you ask PRYNOVA.NET to delete your SpeedCash account and the personal data tied to it. You can do that yourself in the app, or by writing to us.

How to delete the account in the app

  1. Open the SpeedCash app and sign in with your phone number and PIN.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Scroll to Delete Profile.
  4. Confirm. The account is closed and you are signed out.

If Delete Profile is greyed out, you still have an outstanding or active SpeedCash loan. Repay that balance first, then delete. We will not close an account that still owes money.

How to ask PRYNOVA.NET to delete it

If you cannot use the app, email PRYNOVA.NET at speedcashghana@gmail.com or call +233 53 714 4904. Use the subject “Delete my SpeedCash account” and include the phone number on the account. We will confirm it is you, check that nothing is still owing, then close the account.

What is deleted

When the account is deleted we remove from our customer systems:

  • Your login (phone number and PIN)
  • Profile details (name, email, address, work, education, emergency contacts)
  • ID photos and selfie
  • Mobile money payout details stored on the profile
  • The live customer record used to sign in

What we may keep

Ghanaian lending and accounting rules can require us to keep a thin record after the profile is gone, for example that a loan was granted, repaid, or written off. We keep only what the law or a dispute requires, not your ID photos or the rest of the application file. That leftover record is not used to sign you back in.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate once you confirm and you are not owing. Email requests are handled as soon as we can verify the number and the loan status, usually within a few working days.
