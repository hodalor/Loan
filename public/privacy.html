SpeedCash Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Privacy · Terms & Conditions

SpeedCash is a mobile lending service. This page explains what we take from you, why we need it, and how you can ask us to remove it. If you are not comfortable with this, do not create an account.

Questions can be sent to speedcashghana@gmail.com or +233 53 714 4904.

What you give us

When you register, we keep the phone number you sign in with, the 4-digit PIN you set, and the email you add to your profile. We also keep the personal details you type into the application: your name, date of birth, gender, marital status, address, education, work, and income.

For identity checks we ask for a government ID (national ID, passport, voter card, or driver licence), photos of the front and back of that ID, and a selfie of you. Those images are uploaded so our team can confirm you are the person applying.

You also enter emergency contacts yourself — name, phone, relationship, and address. We do not copy your whole phone book. We only store the contacts you type or choose for the application.

If you take a loan or make a repayment, we keep the loan amount, outstanding balance, due dates, payment method, and the history of those transactions. That record is how we know whether an account is clear or still owing.

How we use it

This information is used to open and protect your account, verify your identity, review a loan, send funds, collect repayment, and stop fraud. We look at it when you write to support, and when we need to reach an emergency contact because a loan is overdue.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it to run ads.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or photo library only when you are adding ID images or a selfie. We upload the pictures you confirm, not the rest of your gallery. Those files go to our servers over HTTPS so we can complete verification.

Payments

The account and amount you use to repay a loan are sent to our payment provider so that one transaction can go through. We do not pass your ID photos, selfie, or emergency contacts to that provider.

Who else can see it

Our staff see your file in order to review applications, handle repayments, and answer support requests. We may also have to show records if a law, regulator, or court requires it. Outside of that, we do not hand your information to other companies for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your account for as long as it is open, and for as long as a loan on it is still outstanding. If a loan has been repaid, we may still hold the history needed for records, disputes, and legal obligations. We do not keep data longer than we have a reason to hold it.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Access to customer files is limited to people who need it for lending, collections, or support. No system is perfect, so we cannot promise that a determined attacker will never get through, but we treat your file as confidential and we do not leave it sitting in the app after you log out.

Closing your account

You can delete your own profile in the app: open Profile and tap Delete Profile. If you still owe money on an active loan, deletion is blocked until that balance is cleared. Once the account is deleted, we remove the profile from our customer systems. We may keep a limited record where the law says we must, for example to show that a loan was repaid or that an account was closed.

You can also write to us at speedcashghana@gmail.com if you cannot use the in-app option.

Children

SpeedCash is for adults. We do not knowingly open accounts for anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has registered, we delete that account.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of SpeedCash after that date means you are using the service under the revised policy.
